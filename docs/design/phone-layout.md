# Phone layout

The layout of the browser page on a phone: one column with tabs at its foot instead of the side bar and the three columns, for a phone on the home network ([browser-server-lan.md](browser-server-lan.md)). It is the same app: the same stores, the same requests, the same backend calls; only the composition differs.

## Behaviour

- `usePhoneLayout` (`frontend/src/state/layout.ts`) shows it when the window matches `PHONE_QUERY` (narrower than 768 px, or shorter than 501 px with touch, a phone on its side) and the client is not the desktop app's (`backend.kind` `tauri`, whose window can be 720 px wide). It follows the window as it changes; the phone has no switch to the desktop layout. `frontend/src/app/App.tsx` then renders `PhoneShell` in place of the desktop shell; the desktop dialogs and docks are not mounted there, and the assistant has its own screen (below).
- `PhoneShell` (`frontend/src/app/phone/`) has a title bar (the app's mark, the job being drawn, the assistant; the tab's name is at the foot, and in the bar only for a screen reader), the open tab and the tabs at the foot (`PHONE_TABS`: Generate, Gallery, Drafts, Queue, Models, Browse; the Queue tab carries the count of jobs waiting and running). A tab once opened stays mounted and hidden while another shows, as the desktop screens do. On mount it sets the Generate screen and the image media as the active builder, so `invoke` builds the Generate tab's request.
- The job being drawn (`useGeneratingItems`, gallery-view.md) shows in the title bar with its picture in progress and step; pressing it opens it full screen (`PhoneView`, the viewer's `LiveView`). When the job ends, the full screen shows the picture it made, or closes when it made none (failed, cancelled).
- Generate tab (`PhoneGenerate`): the desktop's left panel itself (`ParametersPanel`, tab `generate`), so every control and its order are the desktop's: presets, the seed and Generate row, the model card, the prompts (presets, dynamic prompts, describe a picture, token counts, the recommended values), reference images, and the image, generation, high-resolution fix and advanced sections. `phone.css` lays it out as one scrolling column with the seed and Generate row kept at the top; the dialogs the panel opens are mounted in `PhoneShell`. The dynamic prompts popover opens below its button on a phone.
- Gallery tab (`PhoneImages`; the phone's name for the boards and the pictures together, as the whole right panel is on the desktop): the board (`selectBoard`, as the desktop's boards list; archived boards only when the gallery shows them), the count, and the board's pictures three to a row in the gallery's order (`usePhoneImages`: the images view, without the desktop's search and date filter), the jobs drawn for the board first. Cells are built a page (`PAGE`) at a time as the end of the list comes into sight.
- A picture full screen (`PhoneView`): its place in the board, a pinch zooms around the fingers, one finger pans a zoomed picture, a sideways swipe of a fitted one or the side buttons step to the neighbour, a double tap zooms in and out (`gestures.ts`, `usePictureGestures.ts`). The info button shows its prompts, model, seed and size. The menu button opens the desktop's picture menu (`SingleImageMenu` with `phone`, which leaves out what only goes to a screen the phone has not: the viewer, compare, workflows, canvas, video, text, upscaling, segmenting, pose from a picture); a long press on the picture opens it too. The buttons at the foot are the desktop's: star (`setStarred`), download (`downloadImages`), use its settings (`recall` all, then the Generate tab), delete (`requestDeleteImages`, asking first when Settings say so). A picture deleted while shown gives way to the one now in its place, or closes after the last.
- Queue tab (`PhoneQueue`): the processor's state with pause or resume and tidying the finished jobs (`pruneQueue`), and every job as a card, newest first: its picture (the picture in progress while drawn), state, step, where it goes when not the gallery, prompt, model and size, and the error of a failed one. A card opens a job being drawn for the gallery or the picture of a finished one full screen. A job waiting or running can be cancelled (`cancelQueueItem`), one failed or cancelled tried again (`retryQueueItem`).
- Drafts tab: the desktop's `DraftsView` (its own model, count and position, the groups, choosing and finishing), laid out in one column with two candidates to a row and its finish bar kept at the foot. Given `onOpen`, each candidate gets a button that opens its picture full screen here instead of the desktop viewer; a draft is a helper picture outside the board, so it has no place and no neighbours there.
- Models and Browse tabs: the desktop's `ModelManager` and `ModelBrowser`; `phone.css` turns the manager's category list into a strip. Browse's controls (source, kind, search, filters, card size) fold behind one menu button (`BrowseBar`, through `isPhoneLayout`) and open in a part of the screen that scrolls; the card size (`useBrowse`'s `cardSize`, starting smaller on a phone) sets the grid's column width on every layout. A model's detail (`ModelDetail`) takes the whole tab, as Browse's drawer does, with its close control in a bar above it.
- The prompt book (the Presets button under a prompt, `PromptBookDialog`) is a screen of its own on a phone, not the dialog: a portal over the layout with a back control, the categories as a strip and the register form stacked above the list.
- Assistant (`PhoneChat`, `frontend/src/features/agent/`): the title bar's button opens it (`useAgent`'s `open`, so a permission question reopens it) over the whole layout, as a phone's chat apps have it, rather than as a seventh tab: the six tabs fill the foot, and the chat brings its own message box there. A picture opened full screen goes over it. A top bar (back, the chat's title, the model and effort of a CLI, past chats as a sheet from the side, a new chat), the conversation (the user's messages as bubbles on the right, the answers as text across the screen, thoughts, pictures, chips with undo, the permission question) and the message box at the foot; they are the window's own parts (`ChatMessages`, `Composer`, `SessionList`). The box grows with its text and Enter starts a new line; the screen follows the visual viewport, so the box stays above the keyboard. Once opened it stays mounted, keeping the draft. Not set up, it says so (sign-in and a server's settings are the PC's: `agent_login` and `agent_endpoint_*` are not served to a phone), with no form; a server's model picker is left out for the same reason. Tools act on this page's stores as on the desktop; one that opens a screen the layout has not still changes its store, and when the request ends `PhoneShell` sets the Generate screen and the image media as the builder again.
- Notices rise from the foot, just above the bars of what is in front (the tabs, the drafts' finish bar, a picture's actions or the chat's message box; `PhoneShell` marks the tab and an open picture for `phone.css`), so they hide neither the job in the title bar nor a tab's first controls. Only the newest shows; the older ones wait under it until they time out.
- Fields are at least 40 px tall (menus 44 px, scrolling inside the screen).
- No keyboard rises by itself: opening a popover leaves focus where it was (`PopoverContent`, through `isPhoneLayout`), and the search boxes of the pickers and of the Hugging Face lookup are not auto-focused there; the keyboard comes when a field is tapped. The big prompt editor does not focus its field on a phone either and stacks the two prompts. Every dialog but the small confirmations (`ui-modal--sm`) takes the whole screen (`phone.css`), and those that show a picture with its settings beside it (fix the face, remove the background, describe as a prompt, select a part, pose) put the settings under the picture. A LoRA's trigger words and a reference image's settings open in the middle of the screen (`phone.css` pins Radix's wrapper), not beside their button. Every text field of the layout, those of popovers and dialogs included, is 16 px or more (one rule in `phone.css`), because iOS Safari zooms the page into a smaller one when it gets focus. The viewport keeps pinch zoom.

## Boundaries

None of its own: it calls the commands the desktop layout calls, through the same client.

## Known limits

- Everything outside its tabs (canvas, workflows, comic, extensions, settings) needs the desktop layout (a computer), and so do signing the assistant in and its server's settings. The assistant's request goes from the page that sent it: a request from the PC is not followed on the phone.
- A job's details (its batch, times and parameters) and cancelling or clearing many at once (a batch, everything but the current job, the whole queue) need the desktop layout.
- Choosing several pictures, comparing and the picture actions that go to a desktop screen need the desktop layout. Starring a picture moves it to the front of a board that sorts starred first, so its place changes.
